Boomdrum

Support

Boomdrum is an iPad app for learning and practicing drums on an electronic drum kit: notes fall toward each drum, you play along, and the app scores your timing.

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email steve0zhao@gmail.com. Tell me which iPad you use and, if it's about your kit, its brand and model.

What you need

  • An iPad with iPadOS 15 or later.
  • An electronic drum kit that sends MIDI, connected with a USB cable (through a USB-C or Lightning adapter) or over Bluetooth MIDI. Without a kit you can still try the app with the on-screen pads or a keyboard.

Connecting your kit

Plug the kit's module into the iPad, then open Settings › Drum kit. A connected kit is listed there. For a wireless kit, tap Bluetooth MIDI to pair it. If a pad shows up as the wrong drum, use Pad check: hit the pad and pick the right drum.

Hits are early or late

Wireless headphones and Bluetooth kits add delay. Use Settings › Timing to adjust latency until your hits land on time, or use wired headphones.

Keeping your progress safe

Everything is stored only on your iPad. Use Settings › Backup › Save backup now and then and keep the file in Files or iCloud Drive. On a new iPad, install Boomdrum and choose Restore from backup.

Scanning a printed sheet

Photograph the page straight on, in good light, with the whole page in the frame. Measures the app isn't sure about are marked yellow so you can check them against the picture before saving.
