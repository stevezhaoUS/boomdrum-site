Boomdrum

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Your profiles, practice results and imported music stay on your iPad. Boomdrum has no app account or developer analytics service. The ad-supported release uses Google AdMob to display non-personalized ads and process advertising, device and diagnostic information. Apple handles Plus purchases. Boomdrum Plus removes all import and practice ads.

Boomdrum is an iPad drum practice app made by Steve Zhao. This policy covers the app and this support website. The advertising sections apply to the upcoming ad-supported release; earlier releases without an advertising SDK do not request ads.

Information kept on your iPad

  • Profile names and pictures, age range and experience level. Age and experience suggest a starting difficulty.
  • Practice scores, timing, stars, progress and drum hits.
  • Imported MIDI, MusicXML, PDFs, images and backing audio, and charts you create.
  • Settings, drum-kit mapping, latency, cached Plus ownership, free-import usage and practice-ad timing.

I do not operate a server that receives this information. Boomdrum does not send profiles, ages, practice scores, scanned sheets or music files to Google or Apple as part of advertising or purchases. Sheet recognition and practice run on the device. Deleting the app removes its local data; exported copies remain wherever you saved them.

Advertising and Google services

The free version includes three successful ad-free MIDI, PDF or image imports shared across these formats. After that, you may watch a rewarded ad before an import. Practice interstitial ads are requested only at safe boundaries, before a fresh practice run or after leaving practice, after about 25 minutes of active practice. MusicXML imports remain ad-free.

When an ad is requested, Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) may transmit and process:

  • IP address and approximate region, including region used to determine privacy-message requirements.
  • Device or app identifiers and technical details such as operating system and device characteristics.
  • Ad impressions, video views, reward events, clicks and other interactions.
  • Crash logs, performance information and other diagnostics.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, operate privacy controls and analyze or improve its services. Some data may be associated with device or app identifiers even though Boomdrum has no user login. Advertising buyers may receive information needed to deliver and measure ads. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Boomdrum requests non-personalized ads with child age treatment and a General content rating for all users. It disables the SDK's coarse-location collection and publisher first-party ID, does not request location or App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not request access to your advertising identifier (IDFA). These settings do not eliminate network, fraud-prevention or diagnostic processing. Apple's SKAdNetwork may provide privacy-preserving ad attribution.

The advertising SDK is initialized when an ad is first requested, rather than at app launch. When verified Plus ownership is available, Boomdrum skips ad requests. Purchasing Plus prevents future import and practice ad requests; it does not retroactively delete information already processed by Google.

Privacy choices and retention

Where Google requires a privacy message, Boomdrum uses UMP before requesting ads. When required, a Privacy choices entry in Settings lets you reopen Google's form. You can decline a rewarded ad and cancel that import, or purchase Plus to remove ads. Core lessons and practice do not require sharing your locally stored practice content.

Local data stays until you remove it or delete the app. Google and Apple control retention of data processed by their services under their policies; I do not have access to an individual user's advertising records. For privacy questions, contact me at the address below. You can also use Google's and Apple's privacy tools to request information or exercise applicable access, deletion or other privacy rights.

Boomdrum Plus purchases

Plus is a one-time non-consumable purchase processed through Apple StoreKit. Buying, restoring and checking ownership may communicate with Apple. Apple handles your Apple Account, payment information and transaction records under Apple's privacy policy. I do not receive your payment details or Apple Account credentials. The app uses Apple-verified entitlements and caches ownership locally. Plus ownership and ad-usage counters are excluded from Boomdrum's export backups; ownership is checked with Apple.

Backups and permissions

Settings > Backup exports your local practice data to a location you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. That service handles the file under its own terms. Boomdrum reads a backup only when you choose to restore it.

Camera and file access are used only for sheets, photos or files you select. Bluetooth MIDI connects your drum kit; hits are processed locally. Boomdrum does not request microphone, contacts or location access. Opening an advertiser's website or app is governed by that advertiser's privacy policy.

Children and families

Profiles and children's practice information remain on the device. The app does not send a child's profile name, photo or age to Google. All ad requests use child age treatment, non-personalized ads and General content filtering. Google may still process the technical information described above to operate, measure and protect the advertising service. Parents can purchase Plus to remove ads. A parental question precedes the in-app purchase flow.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose servers may log IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement. The site has no advertising, analytics scripts or cookies of its own. Built-in drum sounds, lesson audio and fonts are bundled in the app.
