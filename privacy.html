Boomdrum

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

In short: Boomdrum doesn't collect any information about you or your children. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Practice, lessons and sheet scanning work on your iPad without an internet connection. Buying or restoring Boomdrum Plus uses Apple’s App Store services. Your profiles, practice results and imported content are not sent to me.

Boomdrum is an iPad app for practicing on an electronic drum kit. It is made by Steve Zhao ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with information. It applies to the Boomdrum app and to this website.

What stays on your iPad

To work, Boomdrum keeps the following in the app's own storage on your iPad:

  • Profiles: the name and picture you choose for each person, and the age range and experience level picked during setup. The age range and experience are only used to suggest a starting difficulty.
  • Practice results: scores, stars, timing and which drums were hit, for each run.
  • Charts and backing tracks you create or import, including sheets you scan.
  • Settings, such as difficulty, metronome, the drum kit's note mapping and latency.

Boomdrum does not upload this information to me or an app server. It leaves the app only when you choose to export or share it, such as saving a backup. Deleting the app deletes its local data; copies you saved elsewhere remain there.

Backups

Settings › Backup can save everything above to a file. You choose where it goes, for example the Files app, iCloud Drive or another device with AirDrop. From then on that file is handled by the service you picked, under its own privacy terms. Boomdrum only reads a backup when you choose to restore one.

Boomdrum Plus purchases

Boomdrum Plus is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. Buying, restoring and checking purchase availability or ownership may communicate with Apple. Apple handles your Apple Account, payment information and transaction records under Apple’s privacy policy. I do not receive your payment details or Apple Account credentials.

The app reads Apple-verified purchase entitlements and stores a local cache of whether Plus is unlocked, so it can keep working offline. It also stores the number of free sheet scans used on this iPad. These values are not included in Boomdrum backup files; purchase ownership is checked with Apple instead. Boomdrum does not send your profiles, practice results, charts or backing tracks to Apple as part of the purchase flow.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: only when you choose to photograph a printed drum sheet. The photo is read on the iPad to turn it into a chart and isn't kept or uploaded; only the resulting chart is saved.
  • Photos and files: Boomdrum only sees the PDFs, photos, MIDI, MusicXML and audio files you pick to import.
  • Bluetooth: to connect a wireless MIDI drum kit. The notes your kit sends are used to score your playing on the iPad.

Children

Boomdrum is made for families, children included. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. Nothing a child enters is shared with me or anyone else.

Third parties

Boomdrum contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it doesn't sell or share data for advertising or analytics. Apple processes in-app purchases as described above. The drum sounds, lesson audio and fonts are built into the app.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement. This website uses no cookies or analytics of its own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting information, this policy will say so before that version of the app is released.
